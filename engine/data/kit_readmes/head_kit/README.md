# Head kit

**For the Eyes page:** `B31_iris.png` is the game's eye color image. Paint on it for a new eye color, keep its size,
and drop your image on the Eyes page. The eyes' shape never changes.

**The game's head, for reference:** `head_kit.glb` holds the game's head meshes (YOUR_head, REF_mouth, REF_lashes,
REF_eyes), without a skeleton. Your own head is part of your model's skin (see `Amicia Clean slate + weight body`): use
these meshes to place it around the game's eyes and lashes, which are the only game pieces the tool keeps. Nothing from
this kit is exported or dropped anywhere.

`B05_head_skin.png` is the game's head skin, to look at when you paint your own.
